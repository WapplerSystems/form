Errors on the form-root property path - everything a form-level validator reports through $this->addError() rather than ->forProperty() (Challenge, MinimumFillTime, and EntropySpam's fallback when it cannot attribute the text to one field). No field partial renders these, so without this block such a submission is rejected silently: the visitor gets the form back with no explanation.
